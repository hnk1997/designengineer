    FAQ

    The questions I get most. Answered before you ask.

    Who is Hasan Nawaz?

    I’m a design engineering partner in Dubai with 7+ years of experience. I work with founders, agencies and product teams, and I’m 6x award-winning, including Cannes Lions and the Loeries.

    What does a design engineering partner do?

    I help you make the right product and tech decisions, then design, build, QA and launch what we decide, all as 1 person. You talk to whoever is making the calls and doing the work, and what you approved is what ships.

    Can I hire you just for advice?

    Yes. Audits, discovery and advisory are all ways to work with me, one-off or monthly on the Consultant plan. I look at your product, your data or your plans and tell you what I’d do, and you decide who builds it.

    How much does a website cost in Dubai?

    With me, a website starts from AED 18,000 and a landing page from AED 7,500. App UI/UX starts from AED 20,000 and platforms from AED 25,000. The full rate card is available on request.

    How long does a project take?

    A landing page takes about 2 weeks, a website 4–6 weeks, a platform 6–10 weeks and app UI/UX 4–8 weeks.

    Is SEO and AI search included?

    Yes. SEO and GEO are part of every landing page, website and platform, so you can be found on search engines and AI assistants.

    Do you build mobile apps?

    I design them and make a coded prototype, then hand over to your developers. I build the front end of websites and web platforms myself.

    Do you work with agencies?

    Yes. White-label is built for agencies. I join your team under your name, and your client sees your logo, not mine.

    Do you offer monthly support?

    Yes. Maintain starts at AED 5,000 a month for live websites, and there are plans for design and tech advice, agencies and big launches too. Paid monthly in advance. Cancel anytime.

    How many projects do you take on at once?

    2 at a time, so yours gets my full attention. If I’m full, I’ll tell you when I can start.

    What happens if you’re ill or away?

    You’ll hear from me straight away, and we’ll agree a new date together. You see real builds every week, so you always know where the work stands.

    How do we get started?

    Message me on WhatsApp. We have a short call about the problem, then agree scope, timeline and cost in writing before anything starts.
