    What I do

    Research, design, build, QA and launch. Agencies split that across 5 people. I don’t.

    1. Research

      I talk to your users and read your data before I draw anything. You get answers, not assumptions.

      • Interviews
      • Audits
      • User flows

    2. Design

      Interfaces and design systems made for real content, real users and the next 10 features.

      • UX and UI
      • Design systems
      • Prototypes

    3. Build

      Websites and leaner products, built end to end. On bigger platforms, the front end, alongside your engineers.

      • Websites
      • Front end
      • Performance

    4. QA

      Every screen checked on every breakpoint before it reaches you. The bugs meet me first.

      • Cross-browser
      • Devices
      • Accessibility
    5. Live

      Launch

      I put websites and leaner products live myself. On bigger platforms, I launch with your team, then fix what real users find.

      • Release
      • Handover
      • Iteration

    How I work

    No account managers. No handoffs. You talk to the person doing the work.

    1. We talk

      A short call about the problem, not a pitch. If I’m not the right fit, I’ll tell you.

    2. We agree

      Scope, timeline and cost in writing before anything starts. No surprises later.

    3. You watch it take shape

      Weekly check-ins with real builds you can click, not slides about progress.

    4. It ships

      Tested, launched and handed over properly. I’m still a message away after.
